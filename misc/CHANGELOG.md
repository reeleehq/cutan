- **The `an character` sub-commands are pinned here** (with thorwhalen/an#424): a literal list beside `cutan.characters.cli._dispatch_funcs`, so a new sub-command is pinned by the genre and no longer breaks `an`'s CLI test.

- **Kurzgesagt wipes are live** (an#390): `live.transitions.wipe` (`{kind: wipe, duration: 0.5, direction: left}`). The guidance no longer calls wipes unbuilt, and the stale-claim guard refuses a spec that does.
- **Nested chains** (an#340, R3 of an#331): `play.slot_parent`/`slot_node_path` read the stage's `slot_parent_chain`; `suppressed_slots` is transitive under the head slot and the compile pass hands it to `build_rig_subtree(skip_slots=)` (the stage no longer knows the head bone); a `RigError` is a `CutoutCompileError`; `_limb_pair` and the one-part presets (`nod`, `speech_pulse`) find their parts by slot name in a chain (`resolve_part_arg`); the character analyser composes `rig.hierarchy` (0.4.0); `REQUIRED_AN_API_LEVEL` 5.
- **Factory faces read at the style framing** (cutan#61): the factory head's brows rest `BROW_DROP` (8.9 view-box units) lower, clear of the hairline, and draw at `BROW_STROKE` 7.5. Mouth forms curve twice as far (`SMILE_CURVE_GAIN` 1.0) with a `LIP_STROKE` of 3, and `angry` is a default form. New characters get a `HALF` lid (`cutan.characters.lids`), and `angry` glares with it (lids -0.45, preset version 2). At 720p and `stage.scale` 1.5, `angry` 1.0 changes 64 lint pixels (was 28). Existing characters keep their art. The factory digests are re-taken.
- **Stand a character on its feet** (an#285, its asset half): `new_character(feet_origin=True)` / `an character new --feet-origin` declares the rig's `origin` at its root bone (the ground contact), so `stage.at` is where the feet stand and every build placed at one `y` shares a floor line; the default is unchanged (the default flip is an#285's open decision).
- **How big a speech pulse is** (cutan#67): the `cutan` skill states the measured default (the head's top moves about 6 px for about 1.5 frames at 24 fps, scale 1) and how to make it read: `speech: {method: pulse, args: {strength: …}}`, closer framing, or a nod or hop on the line's first beat.

- **The factory records its recipe; `redraw_digests` re-derives its bytes anywhere** (an#292): every outermost drawing call (`new_character`, `add_gaze`, `add_views`) appends itself and every parameter it ran with to `metadata.factory` (`{version, steps}`), and `redraw_digests(descriptor)` replays the steps offline into a scratch folder and returns each file's sha256 (memoised per recipe; 0.04 s for a default character). It can only confirm the factory's output; a DiceBear head (network), an older recipe format, an unknown call or a parameter JSON cannot hold confirm nothing. Exposed to the core as the `credits.factory_redraw` service. Hardened by an independent review: a replay takes only the parameters each call records (no `overwrite`, no folder, nothing unknown), a `name` that is one plain folder name, one `new_character` (never a second drawing) with `use_dicebear` forced off, at most 8 steps and 16 KB of parameters; and it proves only bytes its own writes produced under the factory's stamp (the write log `_record_drawn` reads, now shared as `_stamped_writes`), memoised per recipe AND per loaded drawing code. The golden factory digest drops the recipe as it drops the stamps. Not recorded yet: `an character mouths` (its palette is not in the descriptor).

- **The style lint does not tell a one-shot film to split** (cutan#64): when the authored shot list is one shot, a missed `cuts_per_min` or `mean_shot_s` target is reported as info with the guidance's answer (continuous action on one set stays one shot; cut only where the time or place changes) instead of "split long shots". ## 2026-10-05

- **Docs and coverage** (cutan#23, #24, #25): a legless figure glides, not rocks, everywhere it is said (the descriptor's `gait` field, the art-package skill, a test docstring); `misc/docs/locomotion_gaits.md` says what the gaits do (the hem halves tilt as mirror images, and swing like legs in profile; `waddle` and `bounce` move their legs by view; `rock` rocks about the origin; the `waddle` preset and the `waddle` gait are two things; South Park's `bounce` is declared per character until cutan#9); the gait test walks a `parts` rig with legs beside the legless placeholder, which `WALK_LEG_NAMES`'s comment no longer credits with legs.
- **A walk is never substituted silently** (cutan#17, #21, #22): a `profile` asked while the character shows a view its legs do not swing in is resolved without its side view (the chain, or a policy's next choice: `legs` by default), recorded and warned with the remedy (turn to the side first; the walk's own `view` arg poses the legs without swapping the art, so it does not count); a walk played on a part of a character is refused by `an validate` and the compiler; a legged gait asked of a prop glides, recorded and warned; a gait parameter the walk's gait never reads is an error for an explicit gait (a pinned choice's args included) and a warning for the descriptor's or the chain's.
- **`walk` is version 4** (cutan#14, #15, #16, #18, #20, on top of version 3 from cutan#13; every locomotion method is version 2; every shot that plays a walk re-renders): a one-step walk swings its limbs (their one extreme mid-step) instead of sliding; the `profile` body cycle is phased to the contacts (up before the first contact, down after the last; from three steps on the legs pass each other) and `bob` is its whole travel between two contacts; the sway of `waddle`, `rock` and `hem` leans onto the standing foot whichever way the figure faces (it follows the rest `scale_x`); an amplitude set to 0 writes no channel, so an authored move on that property runs untouched; the body lands with the landing tween, not a settling `set`, so an authored move that outlasts the walk no longer snaps it back.
- **A `sequence` waits exactly as long as the walk runs** (cutan#12): the walk's extent is resolved with the same gait (the author's, the descriptor's, or the chain's substitute) and the same drawn scale the expansion uses (`cutan.characters.methods.walk_preset_context`, read by the compiler's and `an validate`'s extent resolvers), so a descriptor-declared `shuffle`, a substituted gait or a `scale: 2` figure no longer starts the next action early or late.
- **The walk's drawn scale is the stage scale, not the pose at the play's start** (cutan#13; `walk` is version 3, every shot that plays a walk re-renders): `walk` takes `scale`, which the compiler fills from the built rig, so a walk that starts under a `pop_in` no longer crashes with "step_length must be positive" or hops 0 px, and a figure resized by an authored move strides as drawn. A scale that is not finite and positive is refused naming the scale.

## 2026-10-03

- **Locomotion gaits** (an#224, P10 of an#231): `walk` takes nine gaits, each a locomotion method with declared requirements — `legs`, `profile` (the four-pose profile cycle; needs `swap.view:side`), `shuffle`, `hem` (need `limbs.legs`), and `waddle`, `hop`, `bounce`, `glide`, `rock` (need nothing). The chain is `legs` → `glide`: a figure without legs now glides instead of rocking about its feet. Default lengths (`step_length`, `bob`, `hop_height`) scale with the figure's drawn scale (an#224's comment: `scale: 2` used to shuffle). `an validate` warns when a requested gait will not be used (`cutout.walk_gait`), naming what would enable it. `walk` is version 2. Classification: `misc/docs/locomotion_gaits.md`.

- **The rig presets move here from `an.motion`** (an#322): `nod`, `point`, `turn`, `face_toward`, `walk`, `waddle` and `speech_pulse`, with their defaults and the walk's limb names, are `cutan.motion`; the rig-free presets stay in `an.motion`. `cutan.motion.PRESETS` (the core's and the genre's) is the table a `play` resolves in. Moved verbatim: no preset expands differently, so no pixel moves. `an`'s preset tests moved with them (`tests/test_motion_presets.py`).
