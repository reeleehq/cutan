cutan.audio.phones¶

Phones to mouth shapes: an aligner’s phone timings as a Rhubarb viseme track.

A recorded take imported with a forced aligner’s output carries its phones, AudioClip.phones: (phone, start_s, end_s) from the clip’s start (an#491), in ARPAbet (MFA’s English models: HH, AH0) or IPA (mfa:ipa: h, ə). The take’s record states which (AudioClip.phone_alphabet, an#721) and that wins; a clip without it has the alphabet decided once per clip from its labels (phone_alphabet()).

phones_track() is what a lip-sync provider calls: the clip’s track from its phones, or None to align some other way. A word the aligner could not phonetise (MFA writes spn under a name it does not know, and an drops it) has no phones under it; it is spoken by its letters over its own span, as the word-timing rule does (an.audio.lipsync.word_timings_to_visemes).

>>> [(v.time, v.code) for v in phones_to_visemes(
...     [("HH", 0.1, 0.2), ("AH0", 0.2, 0.3), ("L", 0.3, 0.4), ("OW1", 0.4, 0.6)],
...     total_duration=1.0,
... )]
[(0.0, 'X'), (0.1, 'C'), (0.3, 'H'), (0.4, 'F'), (0.6, 'X'), (1.0, 'X')]

cutan.audio.phones.TONGUE_ONLY_INTENSITY: Mapping[str, float] = {'D': 0.4, 'G': 0.4, 'K': 0.4, 'L': 0.4, 'N': 0.4, 'NG': 0.4, 'S': 0.4, 'T': 0.4, 'Z': 0.4, 'd': 0.4, 'g': 0.4, 'k': 0.4, 'l': 0.4, 'n': 0.4, 's': 0.4, 't': 0.4, 'z': 0.4, 'ŋ': 0.4, 'ɡ': 0.4}¶

How strongly a phone’s shape holds against its neighbours in the co-articulation vote (Viseme.intensity; the vote weighs DOMINANCE[shape] x intensity, cutan.compile.coarticulate). Rhubarb’s B codes both the EE vowel (lips spread) and the tongue-only consonants (T D N K…), which barely move the lips (JALI, Edwards et al. 2016: “tongue-only visemes have no influence on the lips”); the letter alone cannot say, the phone can. Keyed by the phone’s base in either alphabet (ARPAbet upper case, IPA as written); a phone not listed is 1.0. Values are art direction (cutan#157): a measurement, not a sourced number.

cutan.audio.phones.phone_alphabet(phones)[source]¶

"arpabet" when every phone is ARPAbet as aligners write it (ASCII, one case throughout, each in an’s ARPABET_PHONES once its stress digit is dropped), else "ipa". Decided per clip: a SAMPA V (the vowel of love) beside a lowercase l is not ARPAbet V. The rule is an’s (phone_alphabet_of()), which says None where it cannot tell; this lip-sync reads that as IPA.

>>> phone_alphabet(["HH", "AH0", "L"]), phone_alphabet(["hh", "ah0"])
('arpabet', 'arpabet')
>>> phone_alphabet(["l", "V", "v"]), phone_alphabet(["h", "ə", "l"])
('ipa', 'ipa')

cutan.audio.phones.phones_to_visemes(phones, *, total_duration, words=(), phone_to_viseme=<function phone_viseme>, char_to_viseme=None, rest_viseme='X', min_gap_for_rest=0.2, alphabet=None, intensity_of={}, takes_next_shape=frozenset({'HH', 'h', 'ɦ'}))[source]¶

A viseme track from phone timings: each phone’s shape at its start (in the clip’s phone_alphabet()), the mouth closed at a silence, at a pause longer than min_gap_for_rest and after the last sound. A word of words with no phone under it is spoken by its letters (char_to_viseme).

alphabet is the take’s recorded one (AudioClip.phone_alphabet); with none, the clip’s labels decide (phone_alphabet()).

intensity_of maps a phone (its base: stress and modifiers dropped) to the Viseme.intensity of its cue, so the co-articulation vote lets a vowel’s B beat a tongue-only consonant’s (cutan#157); pass TONGUE_ONLY_INTENSITY to weigh tongue-only consonants low (off by default: unmeasured). A phone of takes_next_shape (HH) shows the shape of the next spoken phone, kept as its own only at the end.

None when a phone is one phone_to_viseme does not know: a phone set this table was not written for is better spread by its words than guessed.

>>> phones_to_visemes([("ʘ", 0.0, 0.1)], total_duration=1.0) is None
True
>>> [(v.time, v.code) for v in phones_to_visemes(
...     [("M", 0.0, 0.1), ("AA1", 0.1, 0.3), ("P", 0.6, 0.7)], total_duration=0.7)]
[(0.0, 'X'), (0.0, 'A'), (0.1, 'D'), (0.3, 'X'), (0.6, 'A'), (0.7, 'X')]
Return type:

Optional[list[Viseme]]
