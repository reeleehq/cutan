cutan.verify.eyeline¶

The eyeline lint: do two characters in an exchange look at each other?

Two rules, both advisory (a warning is the author’s to override on purpose), both about the SIGN a character faces (scale_x), the only facing a flat timeline carries (cutan.characters.play.facing_at()):

  • Within a shot (shot_findings()): a character speaking to the one other character on screen should face their side of the frame. at: [x, y] places them; a speaker left of the listener who faces left looks away.

  • Across a cut (cut_findings()): a shot/reverse-shot pair keeps the line (the 180-degree rule). A speaks alone in one shot, B answers alone in the next; A should face one way and B the other. Both facing the same way makes the cut jump the line, and the pair read as one looking past the other.

The rules are pure functions over small records (Presence), so they are tested without a store or a scene; check_eyeline() is the adapter from a scene’s resolved timelines, and cutout.eyeline registers it on the genre.

Only a view that SHOWS a direction is judged (DIRECTIONAL_VIEWS: the profile and the three-quarter). A front character looks at the camera, and mirroring it is invisible as an eyeline (gaze, not facing, directs it); a back one looks away from it. Lines are judged at their start AND end, and warned only when the speaker faces wrongly at both: a turn toward the listener during the line is the author’s fix.

>>> a = Presence("ana", direction="right", x=-1.0, speaks=True)
>>> b = Presence("ben", direction="left", x=1.0)
>>> shot_findings([a, b])
[]
>>> [f.entity for f in shot_findings([Presence("ana", "left", -1.0, True), b])]
['ana']
>>> cut_findings([a], [Presence("ben", "right", 0.0, True)])[0].entity
'ben'

The cut-out genre’s eyeline warnings: a speaker facing away from the one character it speaks to, and a shot/reverse-shot pair whose speakers face the same way. Needs the characters store, like the other turn checks (without it the check did not run). Keyword-only parameters are the seams a host registers its own check with (functools.partial): the views that show a direction, what a character faces at rest, and what makes two shots one place.

Return type:

None

cutan.verify.eyeline.cut_findings(before, after)[source]¶

A shot/reverse-shot pair whose speakers face the same way (the line is jumped).

Judged only for a true pair: each shot holds exactly one character, and they differ. The finding is on the second shot’s character, the one to turn.

Return type:

list[EyelineFinding]

cutan.verify.eyeline.shot_findings(present)[source]¶

A speaker facing away from the one other character in the shot.

Judged only with exactly one other character, both placed, at different x and a known direction: with more people the addressee is not knowable here.

Return type:

list[EyelineFinding]
