cutan.verify.body¶

Body continuity: a prop that depicts a character’s limbs must meet that character’s body.

Two checks an validate runs, over every prop entity whose document declares a metadata.body_attachment (cutan.body_attachment):

cutout.body_attachment, once per shot, at the shot’s start:

  • the character is there when the art shows it from outside: a body part whose character is not on stage is a warning. first_person art (the camera IS the character) is the one view that needs the character OFF screen; with it on screen it is an error;

  • the view agrees: the view the art declares is the character’s view (a view set at the start, else its rest_view, else front). Art drawn from the driver’s seat laid over a driver seen through the windscreen is this error;

  • the relation holds, for a prop placed by hand (one placed without stage.at is derived by the body_placement pass and holds by construction): every socket lands within tolerance × the span between the sockets’ bones of that bone; the prop’s size relative to the character is the declared one within scale_tolerance; the limbs are not crossed; a mirrored character carries a mirrored prop. The error says where the derivation puts it.

cutout.body_attachment_consistency, once per scene: the same prop on the same character has the same size relative to the character, and the same anchor on the body in the character’s units, in every shot (the framings must not drift apart at a cut).

Placements are the compiler’s: the entity nodes of the shot’s built stage (an.motion.stage_poses(), built only when a shot holds a declaring prop), with any set at the shot’s start applied.

**Reported as info and not checked (v1):** the geometry while the character is in a view other than its rest view (sockets are declared on the rest view’s bones; a side view moves and hides arms), a rotated prop or character, a socket on a nested slot or a posed bone, art the store cannot measure. Motion during the shot (cutan#98) is not measured here: when the character moves, the body_placement pass lowers the sockets to pin joints (cutan.compile.body.body_attachment_pins()) and this check says, with the time, when the motion pulls one off its bone (_follow_findings()). A view change during the shot (a set view or a played turn) while a prop drawn from the former view stays on is a warning with the time (view_swap_findings()); the sockets are not followed through the new view. Not checked at all: whether the declaration is TRUE of the pixels: the sockets are the art author’s statement (a critic over probe frames is cutan#95).

>>> body_attachment_findings(Shot(id="s"), {})
[]

The cross-shot check as a constraint on the shared interface (cutan#104, an#508): size conservation of a body-attached prop against its character. Lint-only (no solver term): the placement is derived from the character in every shot, so a drift can only come from a hand placement. The persisted name is the check’s, unchanged.

cutan.verify.body.view_swap_findings(shot, stores, *, path='')[source]¶

A character that changes view after the shot’s start while a prop drawn from its former view stays on (cutan#98): the art no longer shows the body from the side the camera sees it, and the sockets are declared on the rest view. One warning per change, with the time, read off the timeline the compiler resolves (a played turn swaps at its own midpoint). A prop that changes its own look (alpha, a swap set) by then is the author’s answer and is not warned about. Never raises.
