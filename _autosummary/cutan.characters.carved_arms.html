cutan.characters.carved_arms

cutan.characters.carved_arms¶

Split a carved character’s one-stroke raster arms at the elbow (cutan#208).

The factory’s cutan.characters.factory.add_forearms() splits an arm it drew itself (an SVG sleeve and a round hand). A character carved from a video frame has arms that are pixels: the OverSimplified persona’s arms are one black stroke each, a ( and a ) beside the body, with no drawn hand. Such an arm can still bend at the elbow, the way OverSimplified draws a figure carrying a cup (the upper arm down the side, the forearm across the front):

  • each arm’s drawing is cut across the stroke at elbow_at of its painted length: the upper part keeps the arm_* slot and bone, the lower part becomes a forearm_* slot on a forearm_* bone hinged at the stroke’s centre line on the cut, so at rest the arm looks exactly as it did;

  • both halves get a round cap of the stroke’s own ink on the cut (the stroke’s width, its median colour), so the elbow reads as a joint at any bend instead of showing the flat ends of the cut;

  • the hand is a slotless hand_* bone at the end of the stroke (half a stroke width back from its last painted row, on the centre line), so stu@hand_r holds things exactly at the end of the drawn arm;

  • the rig turns to nesting: bones, so a forearm follows its upper arm.

Nesting changes what a swap pose hides. Under nesting: bones a slot on a child bone draws inside its parent’s part: the carved persona’s face slot (on a face bone under head) would draw inside the head, so a face pose that hides the head (head: {alpha: 0}: the drawn face replaces the round head) would hide the face as well. split_carved_arms() hoists such a bone to the hidden part’s parent (the face bone under the torso, at the same rest place), as the flat rig drew it, and reports it. A hoisted face does not turn with a tween of the head: tween it as well (stu/torso/face).

Opt-in, one character at a time; idempotent at the same elbow_at. The new part files are derived from the character’s own art and keep its provenance (the descriptor’s source); metadata.arms records "split-carved".

cutan.characters.carved_arms.split_carved_arms(char_dir, *, elbow_at=0.5)[source]¶

Split a carved character’s raster one-stroke arms at the elbow (see the module docstring).

Writes parts/arm_<side>_upper.png and parts/forearm_<side>.png next to the character’s parts, the bones forearm_<side> (hinged at the cut) and hand_<side> (slotless, at the stroke’s end), the forearm_<side> slots (drawn just above their upper arm), and nesting: bones. Refuses a rig whose arms are not raster drawings, one already nested, one whose bones carry a rest rotation or scale, an arm drawing with a declared size, not anchored at the top of its stroke or crossing it twice on a row, and an elbow_at outside 0.1..0.9. Every cut is measured before anything is written. Idempotent at the same elbow_at. Every part path moves under the torso (stu/arm_r becomes stu/torso/arm_r, stu/head stu/torso/head), and a descriptor animation on the torso now carries the arms and the head with it.

Return type:

CarvedArmsReport
