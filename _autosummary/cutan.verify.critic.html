cutan.verify.critic¶

A vision critic for body-depicting props: narrow yes/no questions over probe frames (cutan#95).

The deterministic body-continuity check (cutan.verify.body) trusts the sockets a prop declares: it cannot see whether a drawing’s sleeves really end where its sockets say, or whether hands were drawn from the camera’s side. A production shipped exactly that: a steering wheel with the driver’s hands drawn from the driver’s seat, laid over a driver seen through the windscreen.

This critic looks. For each shot holding a prop that declares a metadata.body_attachment, it probes frames of the shot (an.probe.frames(), the path a render draws), asks a vision model a short checklist of yes/no questions answerable from appearance, chosen by the prop’s declared view (CHECKLISTS: a first-person prop’s arms DO come from the bottom edge), and reports each answer that differs from the one a correct drawing gives as a warning: never the gate. Free-form “does this look right?” is not asked: vision models answer camera-perspective questions at about 34-47% (ViewSpatial-Bench, arXiv 2505.21500); narrow questions about what is visible fare better.

It is optional and costs money, so nothing runs it by default (the issue’s critic=None keyword on the check’s function would not do: that check runs inside an validate, which never renders). critique_body_props() takes the judge (default: an’s paid seam, an.verify.vision.judge_envelope()) and the frame source as keywords. A configured judge that fails is a failure (an error finding), never a silent pass. Every call is recorded in the report (shot, prop, frames, model): what it cost.

Ask each body-depicting prop of project’s shots its view’s checklist.

project: a loaded an.project.Project or its folder judge: judge(frames, *, prompt) returning the reply text, or an envelope

with reply and model (default: an’s judge_envelope(), a paid call, with model)

checklists: questions by declared view (CHECKLISTS), or one list for all at: the probe instants, as fractions of each shot’s length, in [0, 1] frames: frames(project, shot_id, times) -> [png bytes] (default:

:func:`an.probe.frames`, which needs the renderer's headless browser)

model: the default judge’s model; recorded with each call

A finding is (severity, ir path, message): a warning per answer that differs from a correct drawing’s, an info per unsure, an error when the judge or the probe fails or the reply holds no answers. A prop whose character is not in the shot is not asked (a first-person prop’s is off screen by design, so it is).

Return type:

CritiqueReport

cutan.verify.critic.critique_far_plates(project, *, judge=None, checklist=(Question(text='Does the picture show the ground or a road close to the viewer?', expected='no'), Question(text="Are there trees, poles, fences, signs or buildings near the viewer: large, or cut off by the picture's edges?", expected='no'), Question(text='Is everything shown far away: sky, horizon, distant hills or mountains?', expected='yes')), model=None, max_side=1024)[source]¶

Ask whether each far plate of project’s travel holds anything near (cutan#166): a tree baked into a plate stands still while the streams move.

The plate’s own image is judged, not a render: no probe, one call per plate (at most max_side px long; a plane’s fit never crops, so the image holds everything a pan could show). An SVG plate (drawn by hand, its content known to its author) and a plane with no picture are not judged, each said as an info. Only the plates a travel LISTS far are judged; a plane at infinity because its frame is coupled far is not yet (cutan#177). Findings and calls as critique_body_props().

Return type:

CritiqueReport
