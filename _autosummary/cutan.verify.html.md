# cutan.verify

The cut-out style lint: measures a render against a named style spec.

### Modules

| [`body`](cutan.verify.body.html.md#module-cutan.verify.body)       | Body continuity: a prop that depicts a character's limbs must meet that character's body.            |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| [`cadence`](cutan.verify.cadence.html.md#module-cutan.verify.cadence) | Pre-render cadence lint: the scene's timing against its style spec (catalogue B6, an#519 rank 5).    |
| [`critic`](cutan.verify.critic.html.md#module-cutan.verify.critic)   | A vision critic for body-depicting props: narrow yes/no questions over probe frames (cutan#95).      |
| [`eyeline`](cutan.verify.eyeline.html.md#module-cutan.verify.eyeline) | The eyeline lint: do two characters in an exchange look at each other?                               |
| [`regions`](cutan.verify.regions.html.md#module-cutan.verify.regions) | Mouth and body cadence apart: "mouths on ones, bodies on twos" made measurable (cutan#80).           |
| [`style`](cutan.verify.style.html.md#module-cutan.verify.style)     | Style lint: measure a render's cadence, cut rate and palette, and compare them to a style's targets. |
